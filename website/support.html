Frequently asked questions

Where did HockeyNow go after I opened it?

HockeyNow is a menu bar app. Look for the puck icon at the top of your screen and click it to open the app. If you’d like a Dock icon as well, turn on Show Dock Icon in Settings → General.

How do I choose a favourite team?

Open the sidebar, choose Advanced Settings and pick a team under Favourite Team. Your accent colour updates to match, and the My Team tab becomes available. Use Also follow to add more teams.

The notch HUD isn’t showing.

The Notch HUD needs a MacBook with a notch. Make sure Live Notch Overlay is on in Advanced Settings, then open a game card and tick Notch Pin to send that game to the notch. Hover over the notch to expand it.

I’m not receiving goal alerts.

First, check that Goal Alerts is on in Advanced Settings. If my team only is on, you’ll only be alerted to your favourite teams’ goals. Then open System Settings → Notifications → HockeyNow and allow notifications.

Where does the data come from?

Scores, schedules and statistics are retrieved from ESPN’s public NHL data feeds. If a feed is slow or unavailable, HockeyNow shows an error banner with a Retry button and falls back to the games it has saved on your Mac.

Can I use HockeyNow offline?

Yes, partly. Finished games and past schedules are saved on your Mac so they open instantly, even offline. Live scores need a connection. You can remove the saved data under Clear saved games in Settings → General.

How do I change the language or timezone?

Open Settings → General. HockeyNow supports English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano and Suomi, and 16 timezones plus a 24-hour clock option.

How do I update HockeyNow?

HockeyNow checks for updates automatically. You can also choose Check for Updates… in Settings → General, or download the latest version from the Download page.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit HockeyNow (⌘ Q or Quit Application), then drag it from Applications to the Trash. If you added it as a login item, it will be removed with the app.
