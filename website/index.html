Notch HUD

Your MacBook’s notch just got a promotion.

Pin a game and the area around the notch shows the score, the clock and who’s on the power play. Move your pointer up and it springs open into a full game centre.

  • Period timelines with a dot for every goal
  • Live win probability bar in each team’s colours
  • Scoring plays with scorers, assists and power-play tags
  • Pre-game mode with each team’s top performers

More about the notch

Game details

Every shift, in the details.

Tap any game to open it. See the story of the game as it unfolds: scoring by period, power plays, hits, blocked shots, faceoff percentage — plus an interactive win-probability chart.

  • Pin to the menu bar, the notch, or pop out an Overlay HUD
  • Lineups by forward line, defence pair and goalie
  • Penalties, venue and clock at a glance

And so much more

Thoughtful, down to the last detail.

Any day, any season

Browse games by date with an enlarged calendar, or search a team to see its whole schedule.

Rich game details

Scoring by period, penalties, faceoffs, hits, blocks, power plays, win probability and lineups.

Pre-game insights

Last-five form, home and away splits, probable goalies and each team’s top performers.

Six languages

English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano and Suomi.

Your timezone

Local time or any of 16 zones, with a 24-hour option.

Works offline

Box scores and schedules are saved on your Mac, so past games open instantly.

Low Power Mode

Refreshes less often to save battery. Or pick 15 s, 30 s, 1 or 5 minutes.

Automatic updates

Sparkle keeps HockeyNow current — or check manually any time.

Keyboard shortcuts

⌘1–⌘6 jump between tabs, ⌘[ and ⌘] move a day back or forward.
