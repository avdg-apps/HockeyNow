Download .dmg

Up and running in a minute.

Open the disk image

Double-click HockeyNow.dmg in your Downloads folder.

Drag to Applications

Drag the HockeyNow icon onto the Applications folder, then eject the disk image.

Launch and play

Open HockeyNow. A puck appears in your menu bar — click it, then choose a favourite team in Settings.

Updates take care of themselves

HockeyNow uses Sparkle to check for new versions automatically. Prefer to do it yourself? Choose Check for Updates… in Settings → General. You can also turn automatic checks off there.
