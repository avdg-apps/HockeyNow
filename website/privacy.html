Privacy

Your game. Your business.

HockeyNow doesn’t ask who you are, and doesn’t track what you do.

Last updated: October 27, 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. HockeyNow has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics or tracking of any kind. We don’t collect, sell or share personal information.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your preferences — favourite teams, language, timezone, accent colour and alert settings.
  • Saved box scores and past schedules, kept so past games open quickly and work offline. You can erase them with Clear saved games in Settings.

Network requests the app makes

  • ESPN — to fetch scores, schedules, standings, statistics, injuries and team logos from ESPN’s public data feeds. As with any web request, ESPN can see your IP address.
  • GitHub — to check for updates (Sparkle fetches our update feed) and, if you choose, to download new versions.

Notifications

Goal, period, final and game-day alerts are generated on your Mac and delivered by macOS. They never leave your device. You control them in HockeyNow’s settings and in System Settings → Notifications.

This website

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. Downloads are served from GitHub, which has its own privacy practices.
