Menu bar

Always there. Never in the way.

HockeyNow lives in your menu bar as a compact live ticker. Click it to open the full app — it’s one keystroke from anywhere, even over full-screen apps.

  • Three styles: full label, a compact score (“3-2”), or just the puck
  • Smart menu bar text — your team’s score while it plays, its next game when it doesn’t
  • Menu Pin any game to keep it in the menu bar
  • Opens over full-screen apps and across every Space
  • No Dock icon by default — turn one on in Settings if you like

Notch HUD

The notch, reimagined.

On a notched MacBook, HockeyNow wraps the notch in a live score strip. Hover over it and it smoothly expands into a full game centre — then folds away when you’re done.

  • Collapsed: both teams, the score, the game clock and power-play indicators
  • Expanded: period timelines with a dot for every goal
  • Win probability bar in each team’s colours
  • Exact on-ice strength — “5 on 4 · TOR PP”, empty-net and 3-on-3 situations, straight from the NHL’s play-by-play
  • Shots on goal for each team, live
  • Live pop-outs: on a goal, penalty or period end in your team’s game the notch opens by itself, shows a banner for a few seconds, then folds away — as a small strip or the full notch
  • After the final horn: the three stars and each team’s next game
  • Scoring plays list you can scroll, with scorers, assists and PP tags
  • Pre-game: season top performers for both teams before puck drop
  • Click-through when collapsed — it never blocks your menu bar
  • Pin a game with Notch Pin. Turn the HUD on or off with Live Notch Overlay, and choose pop-out behaviour under Notch live alerts in Advanced settings.
  • Stays anchored to the notch above the menu bar, across Spaces and full-screen apps

  • Frosted, translucent design that blends into your desktop

Games

Every game. Any day.

The Games tab is your daily scoreboard. Flip through dates, jump anywhere with the enlarged calendar, or search for a team to see its whole season.

  • Enlarged calendar picker to jump to any date
  • ⌘[ and ⌘] step a day back or forward
  • Search any team playing this year and see all of its games
  • Preseason toggle for exhibition games
  • Your favourite team’s game is shown first
  • Live, final and upcoming games with times in your timezone
  • Venue shown on every card

Game details

Dig into the whole story.

Open any game card for a live, rich breakdown. Everything updates as the game unfolds, and finished games stay available offline.

  • Five tabs: Goals, Play-by-play, Shots, Lines and (after the game) Post-game
  • Team stats: faceoff win %, hits, blocked shots, power plays
  • Interactive win probability chart across the whole game
  • Scoring plays by period, with scorers, assists and the clock
  • Play-by-play of every play ESPN publishes, with goals highlighted
  • Penalties for the game
  • Lines: the real lines once the game is under way, the last game’s lineup before it
  • Pre-game win probability even before the puck drops
  • Pin to the menu bar or notch, or pop out an Overlay HUD

Shot map & xG

See where the chances came from.

A rink map of every shot, with expected goals (xG) for each team. It refreshes every 15 seconds while the game is live.

  • Every shot plotted on a full rink — goals, shots on goal and misses
  • Bigger dot = better chance, sized by the shot’s expected-goals value
  • xG, goals and shots on goal for each team at a glance
  • Each team attacks its own end, drawn in its colours
  • Built from the NHL’s public play-by-play data

Post-game

The story after the horn.

Once a game ends, the Post-game tab wraps it up: the NHL’s three stars and when each team plays next. The notch shows it too.

  • Three stars with headshots and the stat line (“2G 1A”, “.945 SV%”)
  • Next game for both teams, in your timezone
  • Also shown in the expanded notch when the game finishes

Pre-game insights

Know before the puck drops.

Upcoming games come with everything you need to size up the matchup — worked out from the games HockeyNow has already loaded.

  • Team form: each side’s last five results as W / L / OT dots
  • Home and away records for both teams
  • Probable goalies: recent starts, save percentage and goals-against average
  • Top performers slider: scoring, assist and goalie leaders for each team
  • Recent head-to-head history

My Team

A home page for your club.

Choose a favourite team and HockeyNow builds a dashboard around it. Follow extra teams too, and flip between them with one tap.

  • Team header in your club’s colours, with a next-game countdown
  • Playoff race: whether you’re in a spot and by how many points
  • Team leaders among forwards, defence and goalies
  • Injury report for your roster
  • Recent results and upcoming games
  • Season stats: GP, goals for, goals against and difference
  • Season picker to look back to 2016/17, plus a preseason toggle
  • Also follow any number of extra teams

Make it a game within the game.

A whole tab for the fun side of hockey: count down to puck drop with friends, pick winners, and follow your fantasy players live.

  • “As of” calendar — see the table on any past date
  • Your favourite team is highlighted with a ♥

Alerts

Hear about it the moment it happens.

Native macOS notifications for the moments that matter, with fine-grained control over what you see.

  • Goal alerts for every live game — or my team only
  • Period & final alerts: end of each period, overtime or shootout starting, and the final score
  • Game-day alerts: a morning heads-up when your team plays and a puck-drop reminder 15 minutes before
  • Trade, signing and injury alerts for the teams you follow
  • Per-team alert profiles: goals only for one club, finals only for another, or mute a team for the night
  • Notch pop-outs on goals, penalties and period ends
  • Optional goal alert sound
  • Send test notification and a one-click shortcut to System Settings if alerts are blocked

Siri & Shortcuts

Just ask.

HockeyNow works with Siri and the Shortcuts app. Ask a question out loud, or drop an action into your own automations.

  • “Next game in HockeyNow” — when your team plays and how long until then
  • “What’s the score in HockeyNow” — the live score, or the latest result
  • “Standings in HockeyNow” — record, points and place in the table
  • Say any team by name, nickname or abbreviation (“Edmonton”, “Oilers”, “EDM”) — or leave it blank for your favourite
  • Available as actions in the Shortcuts app

Under the hood

Small things, done right.

Offline ready

Offline? HockeyNow shows your saved games, and tells you so. Clear the cache any time in Settings.

Low Power Mode

Refreshes scores less often to save battery.

Auto-updates

Built-in Sparkle updates, with gentle reminders that bring the app forward so you never miss one.

Keyboard first

⌘1–⌘6 switch tabs · ⌘[ ⌘] change the day · ⌘Q quits.

Menu bar native

No Dock icon unless you want one. Opens above full-screen apps.

6 languages

Full interface localization, switchable on the fly.

16 timezones

Show kick-off in your local time or any of 16 zones.

Private by design

No account, no sign-in, no tracking.

Settings that stay put

Your teams, picks, pinned players and alert profiles are backed up on your Mac, so they survive updates.
